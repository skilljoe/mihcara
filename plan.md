# Cara Burke — Personal Brand Website Draft

## Design direction
- **Design movement:** editorial Caribbean modernism — premium black tailoring, warm coral accents, and soft paper textures that connect entertainment leadership to a children’s-book sensibility.
- **Core principles:** confident but human; expressive without clutter; dual-lane storytelling (industry + imagination); clear conversion paths.
- **Color philosophy:** charcoal and warm ivory create authority and breathing room; coral signals momentum and personality; banana yellow and leaf green appear as small sparks that nod to Jamaica and childhood without turning the page into a theme park.
- **Layout paradigm:** an offset editorial canvas with oversized statements, angled color blocks, and asymmetric cards instead of a centered grid.
- **Signature elements:** coral “make it happen” stamp, thin gold/coral rules, and hand-drawn spark/star motifs.
- **Interaction:** anchor navigation, generous hover lift, magnetic-feeling CTAs, and clear reveal of editable placeholder content.
- **Animation:** slow ambient glow/float in the hero, subtle card rise on hover, and smooth scrolling; avoid excessive motion.
- **Typography:** serif display face for warmth and authority paired with a clean geometric sans for utility and labels.
- **Brand essence:** a behind-the-scenes force who turns bold ideas into real-world moments — for artists, families, and collaborators. Personality: capable, generous, electric.
- **Voice:** direct, warm, quietly iconic. Example lines: “Big ideas need someone who knows how to make them happen.” / “From stage lights to story time, every detail carries meaning.”
- **Wordmark concept:** “CARA” in an editorial serif with a small coral star replacing the counter accent; “MAKE IT HAPPEN” as a compact all-caps lockup.
- **Signature brand color:** Cara Coral `#f06755`.

## Project structure
- `index.html` — single-page shell and route metadata.
- `src/main.js` — semantic page markup, interactions, and content placeholders.
- `src/styles.css` — responsive editorial visual system and motion.
- `public/assets/cara-burke.avif` — supplied portrait copied into managed storage path at implementation time.
- `public/manus-routes.json` — route manifest for the home page.

## Implementation notes
- Use a static Vite-style app with no backend because the rough draft only needs presentation and enquiry placeholders.
- Keep final book title, cover, copy, package contents, prices, email, and social links visibly editable until Cara confirms them.
- Use the supplied portrait prominently; keep decorative imagery CSS-led rather than adding generic stock photography.
